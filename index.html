RobotForge

Plain language in.
Measured verdict out.

Describe a robot in plain words. RobotForge drafts the spec, builds its simulation twin, and measures whether the model actually does what it claims.

GitHub repository → Project status ↓

How it works

1Describe

Muse Spark drafts a structured robot spec from your plain-language description.

2Validate

Engineering checks catch impossible or inconsistent numbers before anything is simulated.

3Twin

The spec becomes a digital twin that runs headless in Gazebo Sim.

4Gate

A measured motion test grades the twin against fixed thresholds — pass, marginal, or fail.

The Gate

The twin is commanded to drive 1 metre and turn 90 degrees. Odometry measures what it actually did, and the difference between commanded and measured is the grade.

MeasurementPassMarginalFail
Distance error≤5%5–10%>10%
Heading error≤5°5–10°>10°

Thresholds are printed on every report and never quietly loosened.
