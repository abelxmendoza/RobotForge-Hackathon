Why this exists

On a rover team, you learn the model was wrong in the shop — when time, parts, and patience are already spent. RobotForge moves that moment to your laptop, before hardware, where a mistake is information instead of damage.

02 Confirm the spec — Pathfinder

Every value carries its trust level. Tap a chip to confirm it. The gate can run on estimates, but the report will say so — you are never silently judged on a guess.

03 Mobility Pack — the gate

Seven plain tests. Runs headless anywhere — verification stops being gated by access to the physical robot. Thresholds are printed on every report and never quietly loosened.

Distance: ≤5% PASS · 5–10% MARGINAL · >10% FAIL  |  Heading: ≤3° PASS · 3–6° MARGINAL · >6° FAIL  |  Simulation truth is not sim-to-real proof.
