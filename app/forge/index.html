Talk to the forge

One conversation: describe a robot in words, or attach a photo, a parts list, a spec file, or a 3D scan. A photo of a spec sheet or blueprint is read as a document — printed values transcribed, never inferred from drawing scale — and a scan (.ply) is measured right in your browser. Every stage of every run wears the badge it earned — LIVE (a real model call through the live endpoint), LOCAL (real computation in your browser) or RECORDED (a replayed run, labeled with its date). Nothing wears a badge it didn’t earn.
