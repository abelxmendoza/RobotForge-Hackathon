How a run is graded

The twin is commanded to drive 1 m and turn 90°. Drive and turn are graded against the simulator's true pose. Odometry is graded against that same truth, so a robot whose sensors are wrong about itself can't pass. When something is off, the gate names the spec field to look at first.
