Mini Pupper

Legged · Quadruped
Legged quadruped
Not gated

A robot dog you build yourself: MangDang's open-source quadruped packs 12 servos and a Raspberry Pi into 560 g, and the whole design — CAD, code, docs — is published. The Robodex's smallest dog by far.

Spec

Battery800 mAh, 2S — 7.4 V nominal; MangDang's docs warn that over 8.4 V (over 2S) will fry the servos
ComputerRaspberry Pi
Dimensions209 × 109 × 165 mm (MangDang published specification)
Servos12 custom servos, 3 per leg (12 DOF)
Structure mass noteThe published URDF's link masses sum to just 0.304 kg — structure only; the servos, Pi and battery that make up the rest of the 560 g are not in the model
Weight560 g (MangDang published specification)
Source: MangDang — Mini Pupper published specifications, documentation, and mini_pupper_ros · linkLicense: Apache-2.0 (MangDang/mini_pupper_ros)Spec file: examples/mini-pupper.jsonDimensions, weight, battery capacity and servo count from MangDang's published specifications (launch specification, as also reported by CNX Software and the Raspberry Pi magazine). The 2S / 7.4 V battery figure is from MangDang's own documentation ('battery spec… voltage should be less than 7.4 V'; over 8.4 V 'you'll almost certainly fry all your servos'). The description package's link masses (0.304 kg, structure only) are recorded in the key specs, not passed off as the robot's weight. No official v1 photo is linkable — the repository's photo shows the v2 — so this entry carries a labeled RobotForge line illustration instead.
