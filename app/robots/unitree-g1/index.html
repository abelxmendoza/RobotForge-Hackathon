Unitree G1

Legged · Humanoid
Legged humanoid
Not gated

A 1.32 m humanoid with 29 powered joints, a depth camera and a 3D LiDAR in its head — the Robodex's first biped, and the biggest robot in it. Unitree publishes its dimensions and equipment; the mass breakdown here is grouped out of the official model files.
