How it was computed

Support-polygon statics from the spec's declared dimensions: convex hull of the foot contacts, weakest-direction distance from the centre-of-mass projection to a polygon edge, tip-over angle atan(margin / CoM height). No simulation, no motion — a computation, not a gate run.

Support model: Two feet in double support, each a rectangle 0.152 × 0.066 m (length 0.115 × standing height, width 0.05 × height), centred 0.032 × height ahead of the ankle line at ±0.22 × body width — the take-apart viewer's proportions; the spec publishes no foot dimensions.

Assumptions (all of them, as printed in the report):

  • Foot size is derived from standing height with the viewer's proportions; it is not a published dimension.
  • Double support: both feet flat on the ground. A humanoid in single support, or mid-step, is a dynamics problem this check does not model.
  • Centre of mass sits horizontally at the centre of the body footprint.
  • CoM height is ASSUMED at 55% of standing height — the spec declares no CoM height.
  • Flat, level ground; rigid body; no payload.
