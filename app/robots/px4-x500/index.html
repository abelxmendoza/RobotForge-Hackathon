The PX4 ecosystem's reference quadcopter kit: a 500 mm X-frame, four 2216 920KV motors, a power-distribution board and a Pixhawk flight controller. RobotForge flies this exact airframe as the PX4 Gazebo model (x500), so its geometry here is read straight out of the model's own files.
