Asimov 1

Legged · Humanoid
Legged humanoid
Not gatedStatic check: UNSTABLE — tip margin 3°

Menlo Research's open-source humanoid you can build yourself: 1.2 m tall, 25 powered joints, aluminium and MJF nylon structure, and its full CAD, boards and simulation models published. The Robodex's second biped — it walks, so the wheeled motion gate has nothing to grade it with yet. Its hardware licence is strongly reciprocal, so this entry links to Menlo's files instead of copying them.

How it was computed

Support-polygon statics from the spec's declared dimensions: convex hull of the foot contacts, weakest-direction distance from the centre-of-mass projection to a polygon edge, tip-over angle atan(margin / CoM height). No simulation, no motion — a computation, not a gate run.

Support model: Two feet in double support, each a rectangle 0.138 × 0.060 m (length 0.115 × standing height, width 0.05 × height), centred 0.032 × height ahead of the ankle line at ±0.22 × body width — the take-apart viewer's proportions; the spec publishes no foot dimensions.

Assumptions (all of them, as printed in the report):

  • Foot size is derived from standing height with the viewer's proportions; it is not a published dimension.
  • Double support: both feet flat on the ground. A humanoid in single support, or mid-step, is a dynamics problem this check does not model.
  • Centre of mass sits horizontally at the centre of the body footprint.
  • CoM height is ASSUMED at 55% of standing height — the spec declares no CoM height.
  • Flat, level ground; rigid body; no payload.
