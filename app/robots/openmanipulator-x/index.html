OpenManipulator-X

Stationary · Robotic arm
Stationary robotic arm
Not gated

A tabletop robot arm from ROBOTIS: five Dynamixel servos — four arm joints and a gripper — on a TTL multidrop bus, with 380 mm of reach and a 500 g payload. It never moves from its base plate, and it has no battery at all: a 12 V supply plugs into the wall.

The e-Manual states the 0.70 kg weight excludes the base plate; the official URDF's seven link masses sum to 0.594 kg, recorded in the spec's component masses. The spec's width is the gripper's maximum stroke (75 mm) and its height the radial reach — an arm has no body box; both are labeled with what they mean. Power is a 12 V DC supply, modeled in the spec's battery slot with chemistry 'dc_supply' and zero capacity, because the arm carries no battery.
