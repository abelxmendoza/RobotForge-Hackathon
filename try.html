1. You describe it

“A small indoor delivery rover with differential drive. It is about 40 cm long, 30 cm wide and 25 cm tall, weighs 6 kg, rides on wheels 10 cm in diameter and 2.5 cm wide, ground clearance 3 cm, top speed about 1 m/s. It carries a 2D lidar on top for navigation and runs from an 11.1 V 5 Ah battery.”

The model fills a strict engineering registry — it never writes simulation code. The validator then checks the numbers against each other: [INFO] all: spec passed all engineering checks.

3. The forge builds its twin

The validated spec becomes a Gazebo twin deterministically — same spec, same robot, every time. It spawns headless on a Linux box and waits for the gate.

transport: gz-transport bindings (gz.transport13)
ground truth: gz-transport bindings (Pose_V)
