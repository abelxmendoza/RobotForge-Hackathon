RobotForge · Acceptance Gate Evidence

Measured, not believed.

Every RobotForge verdict is judged against simulator ground truth — where the robot actually went — never against its own odometry alone. These are real reports from real gate runs (Gazebo Sim, headless, 2026-10-26). Thresholds are locked: distance pass ≤ 5% / fail > 10%; heading pass ≤ 5° / fail > 10°.

Reports are written by the gate itself (gate-report.json) on every run — spec, raw measurements, per-check verdicts, suspects. Trajectories are the run's ground-truth position stream.
